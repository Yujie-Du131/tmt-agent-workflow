# Experimental design: EZH2 proximity proteomics under CPI-169

RL cells (human diffuse large B-cell lymphoma cell line) were grown as six
independent cultures and treated for eight days: three cultures with 5 uM
CPI-169 (an EZH2 inhibitor) and three with DMSO vehicle.

From each culture, two aliquots of approximately 1 x 10^7 cells were taken.
Each aliquot was fixed and its nuclei were isolated separately. One aliquot
was labelled by AMAPEX (antibody-guided pA-APEX2 proximity labelling) with
an anti-EZH2 antibody, and the other with a rabbit IgG isotype control.
This gives 12 samples: 2 antibodies x 2 treatments x 3 cultures per
treatment. The EZH2 and IgG samples from the same culture are paired,
because they share the same culture and drug treatment, but they were
processed separately from fixation onward.

Biotinylated proteins were enriched on streptavidin beads, digested on-bead
with trypsin, and the 12 samples were labelled with one TMTpro 16-plex kit
and combined into a single run. Four channels are unused.

The main comparison of interest is the antibody-by-treatment interaction:
how CPI-169 changes labelling with the EZH2 antibody relative to the IgG
control.
