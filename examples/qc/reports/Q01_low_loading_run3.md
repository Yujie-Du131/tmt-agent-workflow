# QC report: Q01_low_loading (run 3)

**Overall:** minor_issues

The 12 channels are of good quality overall. PC1 separates antibody (association 0.999) and PC2 separates compound (0.968). Within-antibody correlations are uniformly high (0.963-0.969), and paired-ratio IQRs (0.546-0.620) and slopes (0.929-0.953) are consistent across cultures. The one clear departure is R2_CPI_EZH2, which is under-loaded by about 1 log2 unit and has fewer quantified proteins. This reduces confidence in the CPI169 EZH2 contrasts and the interaction, and the pair from culture 2 should be checked in a sensitivity analysis. No other label, outlier or confounding problems are indicated.

### 1. low_loading (R2_CPI_EZH2)
- Evidence: raw_median 19.135 (raw_median_z -5.659) against 19.908-20.332 (|z| <= 1.574) for the other 11 channels. n_quantified 1397 against 1458-1472 elsewhere. After normalisation its antibody_corr is 0.968 (z 0.682), pc1_z_within_antibody is 0.518 and the paired slope_vs_other_cultures is 0.953, all in line with the other channels. Its median_ratio is -0.26 against -0.149 and -0.204 for the other CPI169 cultures.
- Impact: Only the EZH2 channel of CPI169 culture 2 is affected. This touches the within-culture contrasts EZH2_vs_IgG_in_CPI169 and the interaction, through the CPI169_2 pair. It also touches the between-culture contrast CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_EZH2, where it is one of three CPI169 replicates. The IgG-only contrasts and EZH2_vs_IgG_in_DMSO are not affected. The channel's profile otherwise matches the other EZH2 channels, so the effect looks limited to loss of signal and of low-abundance proteins. The slightly more negative median_ratio (-0.26) would, if real, push EZH2-vs-IgG in CPI169 down for this culture and bias the interaction (CPI169 minus DMSO) toward more negative values. This is a small shift and not clearly outside the spread of the other cultures.
- Recommendation: Check whether the 63 or so proteins missing in this channel are low-abundance ones and whether ratios for low-intensity proteins in CPI169_2 are shifted. Run a sensitivity analysis for the interaction and the CPI169 contrasts with the CPI169_2 pair, or this channel alone, excluded or down-weighted. The metrics cannot separate a real loss of material (capture, digestion or pipetting) from a labelling or mixing-ratio problem. Peptide-level total intensity or TMT labelling efficiency for this channel would help distinguish them.
