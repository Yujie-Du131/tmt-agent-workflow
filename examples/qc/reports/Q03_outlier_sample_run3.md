# QC report: Q03_outlier_sample (run 3)

**Overall:** major_issues

Eleven of the twelve channels are consistent. Within each antibody they correlate at 0.96-0.97, PC1 separates EZH2 from IgG (association 0.98), and PC3 separates treatment (association 0.861). Raw medians are similar between channels. The paired-ratio slopes (0.865-0.960) show no clear departure. R3_DMSO_IgG is a clear outlier on antibody correlation (0.844, z -23.86), PCA position, and the culture-level paired ratio (IQR 1.348 versus about 0.55-0.60). Its loading is normal and its PC1 position is not intermediate toward EZH2, so the metrics do not support a low-loading or label-swap explanation. They cannot identify the cause. This channel degrades the DMSO within-culture contrast and the interaction, and weakens the IgG between-culture comparison.

### 1. outlier_sample (R3_DMSO_IgG)
- Evidence: antibody_corr 0.844 (antibody_corr_z -23.86) versus 0.960-0.967 for the other IgG channels. pc1_z_within_antibody 18.922 (pc1 19.972, versus 9.99-11.16 for the other IgG channels). pc2 -23.219, while the other IgG channels have pc2 +4.4 to +7.9. The paired ratio for culture DMSO_3 has ratio_iqr 1.348 versus 0.538-0.602 for the other five cultures, and median_ratio -0.008 versus -0.127 to -0.173. raw_median_z is -0.667 and n_quantified is 1436 (others 1456-1470), so loading is not low.
- Impact: The within-culture contrasts that use the DMSO_3 pair are distorted: EZH2_vs_IgG_in_DMSO and the interaction. The DMSO_3 ratios are roughly twice as noisy, so these contrasts lose precision and the DMSO ratio estimate is less reliable. The between-culture contrast CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_IgG is also affected, because this channel is one of only three DMSO IgG replicates and its profile departs from the IgG consensus. This inflates variance and could create or hide IgG differences between treatments. The DMSO_3 median ratio is about 0.12-0.16 log2 higher than in the other cultures. This would shrink the apparent EZH2 enrichment in DMSO and push the interaction (CPI169 minus DMSO) toward more positive EZH2 enrichment in CPI-169. The protein-level direction cannot be determined from these metrics.
- Recommendation: Treat R3_DMSO_IgG as a suspect channel. Inspect its protein-level profile against the other IgG channels, for example for enrichment of endogenously biotinylated proteins or of EZH2-proximal proteins, to separate non-specific or contaminated labelling, an enrichment or digestion failure, and a channel or sample mix-up. Then run the primary analysis with and without this channel, or with the DMSO_3 pair excluded or down-weighted, and report both results. Do not rely on the unfiltered interaction estimate until this is resolved.
