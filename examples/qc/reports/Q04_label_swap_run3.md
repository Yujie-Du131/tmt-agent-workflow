# QC report: Q04_label_swap (run 3)

**Overall:** major_issues

Most channels are well behaved: loading is similar (raw_median_z within about ±1.2), n_quantified is uniform, PC2 cleanly separates compound (0.971), and five of six paired-ratio slopes are consistent (1.375-1.491). The exception is the DMSO culture 1 pair. Its channels fall on the wrong side of PC1 for their antibody, correlate poorly with their labelled antibody group (z about -19), and give a reversed paired ratio (slope -0.935). This points to an EZH2/IgG label swap that biases the within-culture contrasts and the primary interaction. It should be checked and corrected before any interpretation.

### 1. label_swap (R1_DMSO_EZH2, R1_DMSO_IgG)
- Evidence: R1_DMSO_EZH2 has pc1 = -11.948 (pc1_z_within_antibody = -23.366), on the side of the IgG channels (all IgG pc1 are about -11.6 to -12.9). R1_DMSO_IgG has pc1 = +12.609 (pc1_z_within_antibody = 31.144), on the side of the EZH2 channels (all other EZH2 pc1 are about +11.4 to +13.4). PC1 explains 54.7% of variance and is associated with antibody (0.667). Both channels have low antibody_corr: 0.878 (z = -18.66) and 0.873 (z = -19.868), against 0.961-0.970 for the other ten (|z| <= 1.6). Their other metrics are ordinary: n_quantified 1467 and 1472, raw_median_z -0.42 and 0.795. The paired ratio for DMSO_1 is reversed: slope_vs_other_cultures = -0.935 and median_ratio = +0.146, while the other five cultures have slopes of 1.375-1.491 and median ratios of -0.115 to -0.210. ratio_iqr (0.589) is normal.
- Impact: The EZH2 and IgG labels of the DMSO culture 1 pair appear to be exchanged. This corrupts the within-culture contrasts EZH2_vs_IgG_in_DMSO and the interaction, because the DMSO_1 EZH2-minus-IgG difference enters with the wrong sign. It also puts a wrong sample into one of the three replicates for each between-culture contrast, CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_EZH2 and CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_IgG, which adds variance and some bias there. The DMSO enrichment of EZH2 over IgG is attenuated, to about one third of its size if the swap is complete (one of three cultures reversed). For proteins enriched by the EZH2 antibody, the interaction (CPI169 enrichment minus DMSO enrichment) is therefore biased upward. This would mask true CPI-169-induced decreases in EZH2 labelling and could create apparent increases.
- Recommendation: Check the TMT channel assignment and sample sheet for the DMSO_1 pair. The metrics cannot distinguish a labelling or channel-assignment swap from a physical swap of the aliquots at fixation or labelling, so confirm this against the records. If a swap is confirmed, exchange the two labels and re-run the analysis. If it cannot be verified, exclude the DMSO_1 pair (both channels) and report the DMSO arm with two cultures, noting the loss of power. After correcting, confirm that antibody_corr, pc1_z_within_antibody and the DMSO_1 ratio slope fall in line with the other cultures.
