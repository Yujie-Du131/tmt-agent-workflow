# QC report: Q05_treatment_loading (run 2)

**Overall:** minor_issues

The design is well behaved overall. PC1 separates antibody (association 0.999) and PC2 separates compound (0.972). There are no outlier channels: antibody_corr is 0.964-0.970 and the PC1 within-antibody z-scores lie within ±1.4. Paired ratios are consistent across cultures, with median_ratio -0.117 to -0.206, ratio_iqr 0.544-0.609 and slopes 0.932-0.955, so there is no clear differential ratio compression. The one pattern that stands out is a lower raw signal in all CPI-169 channels (about 0.5 log2) together with slightly fewer quantified proteins. This mainly affects the between-culture contrasts and is unlikely to affect the within-culture contrasts or the interaction.

### 1. loading_confounded_with_treatment (R1_CPI_EZH2, R1_CPI_IgG, R2_CPI_EZH2, R2_CPI_IgG, R3_CPI_EZH2, R3_CPI_IgG)
- Evidence: loading_by_level compound: DMSO 20.085 vs CPI-169 19.589, a 0.50 log2 gap. Every CPI-169 channel has raw_median_z between -0.202 and -1.112, and every DMSO channel has raw_median_z between +0.202 and +1.144. The spread within a treatment level is small (EZH2 DMSO raw_median 20.125-20.255; EZH2 CPI-169 19.661-19.759). The gap is about twice the antibody gap (EZH2 19.95 vs IgG 19.725). n_quantified is also lower in CPI-169 channels (1435-1440) than in DMSO channels (1458-1472).
- Impact: The shift follows treatment, not antibody, so it hits the between-culture contrasts (CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_IgG and CPI169_vs_DMSO_in_EZH2), which already have the weakest power because they compare independent cultures. Treatment and total loading cannot be separated here. The within-culture contrasts (EZH2_vs_IgG_in_DMSO, EZH2_vs_IgG_in_CPI169) and the interaction should be largely protected, because each EZH2/IgG pair shares its culture and treatment. The EZH2-minus-IgG raw_median difference is similar in both arms: DMSO 0.215, 0.296 and 0.145; CPI-169 0.121, 0.241 and 0.335. If median normalisation does not fully remove the shift, low-abundance proteins would tend to look reduced under CPI-169 in both between-culture contrasts. The metrics do not show how far the interaction is affected.
- Recommendation: The metrics cannot tell whether the lower CPI-169 signal comes from lower input or labelling efficiency or from a real drug effect on the number of biotinylated proteins. Check pre-labelling cell counts and protein amounts (e.g. BCA) per aliquot, and the streptavidin-enriched yield. Treat the between-culture contrasts as less reliable and check whether their effect sizes depend on protein intensity. Base the main conclusions on the interaction and the within-culture contrasts.
